Privacy Policy for Word Nest

Effective Date: September 27, 2026 | Last Updated: September 2026

Summary: Word Nest is an educational Bengali word search puzzle game designed for players of all ages, including children and families. We do NOT collect, store, or share any personal information. Your game progress remains strictly on your device.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Word Nest ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Word Nest operates with privacy by design. We do not require account registration or login. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Personally Identifiable Information (such as name, email address, phone number, or home address).
  • Precise or approximate geographical location data.
  • Financial or payment information.
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • Device identifiers or advertising tracking IDs.

3. Local Data Storage on Your Device

Word Nest saves your game progress locally on your device storage using an encrypted, offline local database (Hive):

  • Current chapter and completed level numbers (Levels 1 to 160).
  • Stars and coins earned through gameplay.
  • Audio settings (sound effects and background music toggles).

This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any remote server or third party.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Word Nest is family-friendly and suitable for all ages, including children under 13. Because our application does not collect any personal data from any user, we strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy requirements.

5. Third-Party Services

Word Nest does not use third-party analytics trackers or data brokers that monitor your activities across other apps. All font assets, level data, and sound effects are bundled locally within the app installation.

6. Permissions Required

Word Nest requires only standard mobile app execution permissions. We do NOT request sensitive runtime permissions such as:

  • No Location permissions
  • No Camera or Microphone access
  • No Contacts or Storage permissions

7. Security of Your Information

Because all game progress is stored locally on your device, your information is protected by your device's built-in operating system security measures.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or Word Nest, please contact us at:
